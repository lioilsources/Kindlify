Kindlify is a non-linear reader for ancient and classical texts.

Instead of reading page after page, you move through a work by its structure and ideas:
• Navigation bar with books, chapters and passages
• Word cloud of key terms — pick words to filter the sections that contain them
• Summaries for every part of the work, with links that jump straight to related passages
• Reading mode with the original text

The library includes works such as the Tao Te Ching, the Analects of Confucius, Greek and Latin classics and more.

Note: book titles, summaries and texts are currently in Czech and in the original languages; the app interface is available in 45 languages.
